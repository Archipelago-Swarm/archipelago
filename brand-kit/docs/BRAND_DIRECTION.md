# Brand direction

**Core thesis.** Archipelago enables autonomous systems to transfer learned capabilities, not merely observations, so distributed platforms adapt collectively, preserve useful intelligence under node loss, and keep operating without centralized AI infrastructure.

**Positioning.** Defense and autonomy first. Primary scenario: UAV squadrons in a contested, communications-constrained environment (Taiwan Strait geography). The brand language stays broad (search and rescue, robotics, disaster response, edge autonomy). Military themes are the core of the product and the hackathon track: use them confidently, technically and without propaganda styling. (Supersedes the media kit's earlier "avoid military-specific imagery".)

**Visual system.** Dark first. Black ~60%, stone/white ~30%, orange <=10% as accent. Arial for display/body, Consolas for labels and telemetry. Vary the metaphor per asset: operational map, layered architecture, lifecycle, dashboard, timeline, typographic contrast. Do not reuse one node diagram everywhere; do not rely on quadcopter clipart.

**Terminology.** autonomous platforms, UAV squadrons, capability exchange, learned capabilities, recipient-side validation, contested / communications-constrained environments, decentralized learning. Avoid "drone swarm" buzzwording and "agents share data".

**Truth rules.** No invented metrics, scale, results or deployment status. Vultr/TigerData are shown as targets (not provisioned per `agents/STATUS.yaml`). Maps and routes are labelled illustrative.
